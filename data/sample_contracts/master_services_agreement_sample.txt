MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of 02 February 2026 by and between:
Tata Manufacturing Systems Limited, having its registered office at 2 Empress Road, Pune, Maharashtra, India ("Company"); and
Omega Business Process Services Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated in India, having its registered office at 45 Residency Road, Bengaluru, India ("Provider").

1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION
1.1 "Services" means the business process outsourcing, IT support and managed services described in each Statement of Work.
1.2 "Agreement" means this Master Services Agreement and all Schedules and Statements of Work.
1.3 "Force Majeure" has the meaning set out in Clause 14.

2. SERVICES
The Provider shall deliver the Services with commercially reasonable care and skill in accordance with each Statement of Work. The Company may issue Statements of Work from time to time.

3. TERM
3.1 The initial term of this Agreement shall be five (5) years from the Effective Date.
3.2 This Agreement shall renew automatically for successive periods unless the Company provides notice of non-renewal at least one hundred and eighty (180) days before expiry.

4. PAYMENT TERMS
4.1 Provider shall invoice the Company monthly in arrears.
4.2 The Company shall pay all undisputed invoices within sixty (60) days of receipt.
4.3 The Company shall deposit three (3) months of estimated fees as a security deposit prior to commencement.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
5.1 Each Party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other Party's proprietary information during the Term and for three (3) years thereafter.

6. DATA PROTECTION AND PRIVACY
6.1 Provider shall process personal data only for the purposes of providing the Services.
6.2 Provider shall maintain a data breach notification procedure and notify the Company of any breach within seventy-two (72) hours.
6.3 Personal data of Company customers shall remain within India.

7. INDEMNITY
7.1 Provider shall indemnify and hold harmless the Company from any claim arising out of the Services or the Provider's employees.
7.2 Company shall indemnify Provider for claims arising from Company-provided data.

8. LIABILITY
8.1 The Provider's total aggregate liability shall not exceed the fees paid by the Company in the twelve (12) months prior to the claim.
8.2 Neither Party shall be liable for indirect or consequential loss.
8.3 These limitations shall not apply to fraud, willful misconduct or breach of confidentiality.

9. TERMINATION
9.1 The Company may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90) days written notice.
9.2 Either Party may terminate for material breach upon thirty (30) days notice if uncured.
9.3 Upon termination for convenience, the Company shall pay a termination fee equal to forty percent (40%) of the remaining contract value.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.
10.2 The courts of Pune, Maharashtra shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
10.3 Any dispute shall first be referred to a designated senior executive of each Party for good-faith resolution within thirty (30) days.

11. ARBITRATION
11.1 Any unresolved dispute shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. The seat of arbitration shall be Mumbai, India.

12. AUDIT RIGHTS
12.1 The Company shall have the right to audit the Provider's books and records relevant to this Agreement upon thirty (30) days notice, but only within one (1) year of the relevant transaction.

13. IP AND DELIVERABLES
13.1 All work product created for the Company under a Statement of Work shall become the Company's property upon full payment.
13.2 The Provider retains all rights in its background tools and platforms and grants the Company a limited, non-transferable license to use such background IP solely for the Company's internal operations.

14. FORCE MAJEURE
14.1 Neither Party shall be liable for failure to perform due to events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of government, natural disasters, pandemics, cyber attacks and network failures, provided the affected Party provides prompt written notice. If such event continues for forty-five (45) days, either Party may terminate this Agreement.

15. COMPLIANCE AND ETHICS
15.1 Each Party warrants compliance with all applicable laws, including anti-bribery and anti-corruption legislation.

16. NOTICES
16.1 All notices shall be in writing and sent by registered post or courier to the registered addresses of the Parties.

17. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
17.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior understandings.
