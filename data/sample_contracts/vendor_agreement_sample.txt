VENDOR SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Vendor Service Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into on 15 March 2026, by and between:
Tata Digital Solutions Private Limited, a company incorporated under the laws of India, having its registered office at 1 Jeevan Bhima Nagar, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India ("Customer"); and
NovaCloud Technologies Inc., a corporation organized under the laws of the State of Delaware, with offices at 200 Liberty Street, New York, USA ("Vendor").

1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Services" means the cloud infrastructure, platform and support services described in the Statement of Work.
1.2 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by either Party.
1.3 "Effective Date" means the date of execution of this Agreement.

2. SCOPE AND DELIVERABLES
The Vendor shall provide the Services during the Term in accordance with the Statement of Work. The scope of deliverables may be revised in writing by the Parties.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION
3.1 This Agreement shall have an initial term of three (3) years commencing on the Effective Date.
3.2 This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year periods unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days prior to the end of the then-current term.
3.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for material breach upon thirty (30) days written notice if the breach remains uncured.
3.4 The Customer may not terminate this Agreement for convenience. All fees paid or payable shall remain non-refundable upon any termination.

4. FEES AND PAYMENT
4.1 Customer shall pay Vendor monthly fees as set out in the Statement of Work.
4.2 Fees shall be paid within fifteen (15) days of receipt of invoice.
4.3 Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of two and one half percent (2.5%) per month.
4.4 Customer shall pay one hundred percent (100%) of annual fees in advance at the beginning of each contract year.

5. INDEMNIFICATION
5.1 Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Customer from and against any and all claims, losses, liabilities, damages, costs and expenses arising out of or in connection with the Services, including claims of third parties, regardless of cause.
5.2 Customer shall indemnify Vendor against all claims arising out of Customer data.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
6.1 The Vendor shall have no liability for indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages.
6.2 Vendor's aggregate liability shall not exceed ten percent (10%) of the total fees paid by Customer in the preceding twelve months.
6.3 The Customer's liability shall be unlimited.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
7.1 Each Party shall protect the Confidential Information of the other Party.
7.2 Confidentiality obligations shall survive for a period of two (2) years following termination.

8. DATA PROTECTION
8.1 Customer data may be transferred to servers located in the United States for processing.
8.2 Vendor shall comply with applicable data protection laws to the extent required.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York, United States of America.
9.2 Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be resolved by arbitration seated in Singapore in accordance with the SIAC Rules.
9.3 Either Party may seek injunctive relief from any court of competent jurisdiction.

10. AUDIT RIGHTS
10.1 Customer shall have no audit rights over Vendor's systems, books or records.

11. FORCE MAJEURE
11.1 Neither Party shall be liable for delays caused by acts of government, war, or natural disasters.

12. ASSIGNMENT
12.1 Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

13. NON-COMPETE
13.1 For a period of twenty-four (24) months following termination, the Customer shall not engage any business in direct competition with the Vendor anywhere in the world.

14. INSURANCE
14.1 Vendor shall maintain commercially reasonable insurance coverage during the Term.

15. COMPLIANCE
15.1 Each Party shall comply with all applicable laws in performing this Agreement.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
